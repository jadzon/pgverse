Swarm Intelligence is the property of a system whereby the collective behaviours
of (unsophisticated) agents interacting locally with their environment cause coherent
functional global patterns to emerge. SI provides a basis with which it is possible
to explore collective (or distributed) problem solving without centralized control or
the provision of a global model.
